## 🚀 Quick Setup for Google Business Profile

1. Visit [Google Business Profile Creation](https://business.google.com/create).
2. Use the fields outlined in [`google-business-profile/PROFILE_DATA.md`](file:///home/runner/work/chante-win-agency/chante-win-agency/google-business-profile/PROFILE_DATA.md).
3. If operating without a commercial retail storefront, configure as a **Service-Area Business (SAB)** following [`google-business-profile/VERIFICATION_GUIDE.md`](file:///home/runner/work/chante-win-agency/chante-win-agency/google-business-profile/VERIFICATION_GUIDE.md) to keep your residential/mailing address private and prevent suspension.
