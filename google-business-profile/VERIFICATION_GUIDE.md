# Step-by-Step Google Business Profile Setup & Verification Guide

Follow this walkthrough to register and verify **Chante Win Agency LLC** on Google.

---

## Step 1: Start Registration

1. Go to [business.google.com/create](https://business.google.com/create).
2. Sign in with the Google Account you want to own and manage the business (preferably a professional Google Workspace account or dedicated business email).
3. In **Business Name**, enter: `Chante Win Agency LLC`.
4. In **Business Type / Category**, select your primary category (e.g., `Marketing Agency`, `Business Management Consultant`, or `Property Management Company`).

---

## Step 2: Critical Choice — Physical Location vs Service-Area Business

> [!WARNING]
> Google aggressively suspends accounts that list residential home addresses, P.O. Boxes, UPS Stores, or virtual office addresses as public walk-in locations.

- **Option 1: You have a physical commercial office with permanent signage**
  - Choose: *"Yes, show my address to customers"*.
  - Enter your physical office address.
  - Ensure permanent exterior/interior signage displays "Chante Win Agency LLC".

- **Option 2: You work from home, remotely, or visit clients at their locations (Recommended for most LLCs)**
  - Choose: *"No, I deliver goods and services to my customers"* (Service Area Business).
  - Enter your service areas (e.g., your city, metro area, neighboring counties, or state).
  - Google will ask for your mailing address for verification purposes, but **it will NOT be displayed publicly on Google Maps**.

---

## Step 3: Add Contact Details & Website

- **Phone Number:** Add a dedicated business line that rings to you.
- **Website:** Link to your website or GitHub Pages landing page. Having a live website that lists `Chante Win Agency LLC` dramatically boosts instant verification rates.

---

## Step 4: Verification Methods & Preparation

Google will automatically present one of the following verification methods:

### Method A: Video Recording Verification (Most Common in 2024–2026)
If Google prompts you for a continuous 1–2 minute video:
1. **Location:** Film the street name, outside building, and house/suite number.
2. **Business Proof:** Film tools of your trade, unlocked workspace, laptop logged into your business dashboard, and official documents.
3. **Legal Proof:** Have your state LLC registration certificate, official bank statement, or utility bill showing `Chante Win Agency LLC` ready to show in the video.
4. **Key Tip:** Do not edit or pause the video. It must be one single continuous recording.

### Method B: Phone or Email Verification (Instant)
If your Google account has established trust or your domain matches your email (e.g. `info@youragency.com`), Google may offer a 6-digit code via SMS or email.

### Method C: Postcard Verification (5–14 Business Days)
If prompted for a postcard:
1. Confirm the mailing address.
2. Do not change business name or address while waiting for the postcard, or the PIN will be invalidated.
3. Once received, enter the 5-digit PIN at [business.google.com](https://business.google.com).

---

## Step 5: Post-Verification Optimization

Once verified:
1. Complete 100% of the profile details using `PROFILE_DATA.md`.
2. Upload high-resolution business photos:
   - Logo (square format, at least 720x720px)
   - Cover photo (landscape format, 1024x576px)
   - Workspace / team / branded visuals
3. Create your first Google Update post announcing the launch of Chante Win Agency LLC.
4. Generate your short review link under **Ask for reviews** and gather your initial testimonials.
