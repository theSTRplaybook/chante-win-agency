# Google Business Profile (GBP) Data Kit: Chante Win Agency LLC

Use this copy-paste profile kit when registering your business on [Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com/create).

---

## 1. Core Business Information

| Field | Recommended Value | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Exact Business Name** | `Chante Win Agency LLC` | Must match your LLC registration / official documents exactly. Do not stuff keywords here to avoid suspension. |
| **Primary Category** | `Marketing Agency` *(or `Real Estate Agency` / `Consulting Agency` depending on your focus)* | Primary category carries the highest SEO weight. |
| **Secondary Categories** | `Advertising Agency`, `Internet Marketing Service`, `Business Management Consultant`, `Property Management Company` | Add 2–4 relevant categories that match your exact service offerings. |
| **Business Model** | **Service-Area Business (SAB)** (unless you have a commercial office with permanent signage) | If operating from a home office or virtual address, **hide your physical address** and select service areas. Google suspends profiles that list residential or virtual mailbox addresses as public storefronts. |
